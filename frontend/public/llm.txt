# AudioTrackDown

AudioTrackDown is a free, web-based media utility designed to extract original high-quality audio tracks, multi-language dubbed voice tracks, and closed caption files (subtitles) from YouTube and Facebook URLs.

## Features

- **Multi-Language Dubbed Audio Extraction:** Downloads separate dubbed audio tracks (e.g. Spanish, Hindi, or French voiceovers) from videos that support YouTube Multi-Audio.
- **Accurate Subtitle Downloader:** Extracts official YouTube closed captions and manual uploads as SRT, VTT, or JSON files.
- **Microsecond Timings:** All subtitle downloads preserve precise, original video timecodes for easy sync inside video editors (Premiere, Resolve, CapCut).
- **Auto-Translation:** Supports auto-generated closed caption translations into 157+ international languages.
- **Privacy-First & Secure:** No advertisements, no malware redirects, and no account signup required.

## API Endpoints

- `GET /api/subtitle/info?url={video_url}`: Fetches video metadata and lists 157+ available subtitle languages, distinguishing between original and auto-generated captions.
- `GET /api/subtitle/download?url={video_url}&lang={lang_code}&fmt={srt|vtt|json}`: Downloads and formats caption files directly.
- `POST /api/extract`: Parses video links to extract all available multi-audio tracks.
